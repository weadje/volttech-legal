Common questions

How do I cancel my Pro subscription? Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → VoltTech Pro → Cancel Subscription. Apple handles all subscription billing — VoltTech AI never charges your card directly.
How do I restore a previous purchase? Open the app → Profile → Settings → Restore Purchases. This re-checks your Apple ID for any active VoltTech Pro subscription. You may need to be signed in with the same Apple ID you originally subscribed with.
Volt AI isn't responding. Volt requires an internet connection. If you've reached your free daily limit (5 chats per day on the free tier), you'll see a message asking you to upgrade to Pro for unlimited chat. The free counter resets every day at midnight.
The Snap-a-Machine camera doesn't work. Make sure VoltTech AI has camera permission: iPhone Settings → VoltTech AI → toggle Camera on. Same for the microphone if voice input isn't working.
How do I reset my progress? In the app: Profile → Settings → Reset progress. This clears your XP, streak, and completed lessons but keeps your profile name and any active Pro subscription.
Are VoltTech credentials officially recognized? VoltTech-issued certificate PDFs indicate completion of curriculum aligned to industry standards (NCCER, OSHA, IFPS, Rockwell, Siemens). They are not equivalent to certifications issued by those bodies. To obtain official certification, you must complete the body's official exam process.
Can I use VoltTech offline? All lessons and labs work offline. Volt AI chat and Snap-a-Machine analysis require an internet connection.
Do you offer plans for shops, schools, or maintenance crews? Yes — tap the VoltTech RME · Industrial Automation & AI at scale card on the Home screen, then tap Get a custom quote. We'll send tailored pricing within 24 hours.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature? Email hellovt26@gmail.com with a short description and (if possible) a screenshot. Bug reports help us ship fixes faster.
