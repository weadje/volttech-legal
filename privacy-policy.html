Privacy Policy

Last updated:

VoltTech AI ("we", "the app") is built to help industrial technicians and controls engineers learn faster. We take your privacy seriously and collect the minimum data needed for the app to work.

Data we store on your device

The following is stored locally on your phone via iOS app storage and never sent to our servers:

  • Your name, learning goal, skill level (provided during setup)
  • Your XP, streak, lesson and lab completion history
  • Your career track selection
  • Theme preference (dark/light)
  • Job-ready opt-in flag

Data we send to third-party services

To provide the AI mentor (Volt) and job listings, we send the following to trusted third-party APIs:

  • Anthropic Claude API — your chat messages with Volt are sent to Anthropic for processing. We do not send your name or other personal info unless you include it in a message. Anthropic's policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Adzuna API — we send job-search keywords (e.g., "industrial maintenance") to retrieve listings. No personal data sent. Policy: adzuna.com/privacy
  • USAJobs API — same as above, US federal job listings. Policy: usajobs.gov/help/privacy
  • Wikipedia API — when you tap "Learn more", the topic name (e.g., "PLC") is sent to Wikipedia. No personal data.

Camera and photos

The "Snap a Machine" feature uses your camera to photograph industrial equipment for AI identification. Photos are sent to Anthropic Claude for analysis and are not stored by VoltTech. We do not access your photo library unless you choose a photo through the system picker.

Microphone

Voice input uses your iPhone's built-in keyboard dictation, which is processed by Apple's on-device speech recognition. VoltTech does not record audio.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use third-party analytics or ad networks. No tracking pixels, no Facebook SDK, no Google Analytics. We do not sell your data to anyone — ever.

In-app purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases (Pro tier, certificate PDFs, resume builder) are processed by Apple's App Store. We do not see your payment information.

Children's privacy

VoltTech AI is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete any associated data.

Your rights

You can:

  • Reset all your progress at any time from Settings → Reset Progress
  • Sign out (clears all local data) from Settings → Sign Out
  • Delete the app — this removes all locally stored data
  • Request data deletion by emailing us at the address below
For EU/UK users (GDPR): You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Most data lives only on your device — deleting the app deletes the data. For data sent to third parties (Anthropic, Adzuna, etc.), refer to their privacy policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version.
